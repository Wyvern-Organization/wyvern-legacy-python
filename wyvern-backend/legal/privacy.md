# Wyvern Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Wyvern Team**, the operator of Wyvern ("Wyvern," "we," "us," and "our"), collects, uses, shares, and stores information when you use Wyvern.

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect:

- account information such as email address, username, display name, and password hash;
- profile information such as avatar, bio, and directory visibility choices;
- content you submit, including messages, uploads, workspaces, server metadata, and webhook-related content;
- operational data such as login events, refresh-token records, IP-based security and rate-limit signals, error logs, and device or client metadata reasonably necessary to run the service;
- settings and preference data, including AI-improvement opt-in state, NSFW viewer self-attestation, and related timestamps;
- public-discovery data when you opt into user or server directory visibility.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- provide, secure, maintain, and improve Wyvern;
- authenticate users and maintain sessions;
- transmit messages, uploads, presence, voice signaling, and related service features;
- moderate content, investigate abuse, detect fraud, enforce policies, and comply with law;
- operate public discovery and recommendation features when users or servers opt in to those features;
- communicate with you about account, security, service, product, support, or legal matters.

Wyvern may send important account, support, product, or legal notices by email. Those messages may come from **support@wyvernhub.net**, **legal@wyvernhub.net**, or another Wyvern-controlled address related to the matter.

## 3. AI-Improvement Opt-In

- Wyvern does **not** use your content to improve AI systems unless you explicitly opt in.
- If you opt in, we may use covered content to improve AI-related product systems and related quality, safety, or evaluation workflows.
- If you opt out later, we will stop using newly covered content for that purpose on a going-forward basis according to the product controls then available.

## 4. Public and Shared Information

- If you opt into the user directory, your profile may become visible to other users through discovery features.
- If a server is opted into the public directory by its owner or admins, server metadata may become visible through discovery features.
- Uploaded media may be reachable by anyone who has the URL, unless additional deployment-layer controls are added outside the core product.
- Messages, files, and other content you share in communities, direct messages, or integrations may be visible to the people and systems you choose to share them with.

## 5. Cookies, Local Storage, and Similar Technologies

Wyvern may use session storage, local storage, and similar client-side technologies to keep you signed in, preserve preferences, support feature routing, and maintain core product behavior.

## 6. How We Share Information

We may share information:

- with service providers and infrastructure vendors who help us host, store, secure, or operate Wyvern;
- when necessary to investigate abuse, enforce our policies, or protect users, Wyvern, or the public;
- to comply with legal obligations, lawful requests, court orders, or emergency disclosures;
- in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, sale of assets, or similar business transfer.

We do not share your information with marketing partners for unrelated advertising under this policy.

## 7. Retention

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, protect users and the platform, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention may vary depending on the type of data and the reason we hold it.

## 8. Security

Wyvern uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no service can guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Wyvern is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly allow children under 13 to use the service.

## 10. Your Choices and Requests

You may be able to update some account information and preferences inside the product.

For privacy requests, data questions, or account-deletion requests, contact **legal@wyvernhub.net**. General support requests should go to **support@wyvernhub.net**.

Wyvern may continue a privacy or legal matter from **legal@wyvernhub.net** or from a different Wyvern-controlled email address associated with the matter.

## 11. International Use

Wyvern is operated from the United States. If you use Wyvern from another jurisdiction, you understand that your information may be processed in the United States and other places where service providers operate.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make a material change, we may require you to accept the updated policy before continuing to use Wyvern.

## 13. Contact

- Privacy and legal matters: **legal@wyvernhub.net**
- General support: **support@wyvernhub.net**
